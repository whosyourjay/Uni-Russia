Funding lists ·

The test is the same; the queues are not.

Budget and paid applicants normally submit the same required ЕГЭ subjects but compete on separate ranked lists. Each point matches the two funding pools inside one university and one HSE broad field; point size follows admitted seats and the diagonal means equal selectivity.

College route ·

College demand is visible; its seat denominator is not.

СПО colleges and technical schools admit primarily on school-certificate grades. Their standardized pages report entrant GPA and applications per budget place, but not admitted headcounts. Each point is therefore a school, never a seat.
